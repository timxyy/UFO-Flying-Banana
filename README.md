This is the sequel to UFO Flying! 
It's a side-scroller where you are a monkey in a UFO who has to balance a hovering UFO while shooting bananas at evil aliens. 
Includes two levels with boss fights and entirely original art and music from my friend Matthew Tsai!
Unfortunately includes quite bad programming practices due to assignment restrictions (tons of hard coding, no OOP), but it shows growth!
